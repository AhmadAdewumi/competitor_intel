**II. Introduction**
--------------------

GPU programming refers to the process of writing software that utilizes the processing power of Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) to perform complex computations. The growing interest in GPU programming is driven by its increasing importance in various fields, including game development, scientific computing, and machine learning. The objective of this report is to provide an overview of the current state of GPU programming, its significance, and its evolving landscape.
